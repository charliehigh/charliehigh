# Charles Okai Addai

Mining Engineer working at the intersection of ore control, geostatistics, mine planning, and digital mining systems.

My work focuses on:
- geostatistical simulation and uncertainty-aware mine planning
- ore routing, dilution and misroute tracking
- stockpile degradation and exposure-risk analytics
- operational dashboards and workflow automation using Python, SQL, and Power BI

I completed an M.S. in Mining Engineering at Michigan Technological University. My thesis developed a multi-loss Wasserstein GAN framework for geostatistical simulation, with applications to geological uncertainty modeling and decision support in mining.

## Featured work
- **Geostatistical Simulation with Multi-Loss WGAN-GP** — thesis code, methods, experiments, and results
- **Mine Misroute / Targeting Dilution Tracking** — data pipeline, SQL extraction, Minesight export workflow, dashboard logic
- **Stockpile Exposure & Age Risk Model** — risk scoring framework for ore degradation and decision support
- **Broken Reserve Dashboard & Mine Analytics** — operational reporting, routing, and production monitoring tools

## Research and practice areas
Mining Engineering · Ore Control · Geostatistics · Mine Planning · Mineral Resource Analytics · Applied Machine Learning · Data Engineering for Mining
